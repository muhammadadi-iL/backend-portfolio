The problem

A home care agency loses the week to the same paperwork: an authorization that expires, a claim that waits, a payroll batch typed in by hand, and a visit whose clock-out was never recorded. Clients, caregivers, billing and payroll have to share one record, or the same fact gets entered twice and the two copies drift.

My role

I built the backend of VALORA, the operating system for that office. It covers clients and caregivers, authorizations, EVV visits, billing and claims, payroll, the inbox and eFax, and the AI agents that draft the work. The caregiver app uses the same API.

Key decisions

A bad visit does not become a bill or a paycheck

A completed visit whose clock span blows past the duration cap is marked needs review. It is not billable, and payable hours keep the clean visits and leave the flagged ones out. A visit that was clocked in and never clocked out cannot be billed or paid until the clock-out is fixed.

The monthly bill follows a verified check-in

A client is billed for a month when three things are true: the month's compliance check-in is verified and not reversed, services were delivered, and an authorization covers the month. Hours come from that verified form. Clock-in and clock-out are only a fallback when the form has no delivered hours, so an EVV gap does not block the bill.

Agents prepare. A person approves.

Each agent has an autonomy mode. Where the mode is approval-required, the draft waits in a queue: an authorization renewal, an invoice batch, a payroll batch. Payroll batches that are still pending stay pending until someone approves them. The action is recorded with who made it.

Portal logins stay in a vault

State portals and payroll providers need credentials. Those secrets sit in a vault. An agent can receive one only for a key it is allowed to use, and the log records the grant without the password or the key.

One client for Claude

Document recognition, an ID scan and a case summary all go through one HTTP client for the Claude API. The model can be overridden per call, and tests replace the HTTP response instead of calling the live API.

Outcome

  • The office, the caregiver API, billing, payroll and the agents run as one Laravel application, live at valoracares.com.
  • A visit with bad time data stays out of the bill and out of the paycheck.
  • Pest covers the visit gate, billing eligibility, payroll, the approval queue and the mobile API. Playwright covers the screens.
