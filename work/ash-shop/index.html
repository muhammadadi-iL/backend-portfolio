Case study · Perfume retail

ASH Shop — Arabic perfumes, in Arabic and English

An Oman storefront for perfume sets: search, a cart, a wishlist, and checkout by card or cash on delivery.

Role
Backend developer
Live

Stack

The problem

ASH Shop sells Arabic perfume in Muscat. Shoppers need the catalogue in Arabic and in English, a way to search it, and a cart that can check out by card or when the parcel arrives.

What I built

A PHP storefront with product search, a wishlist, and a cart. Checkout takes Visa, Mastercard, or cash on delivery. Customers can open their orders, and the shop publishes its shipping and return rules. WhatsApp sits on the page for support. I built it at Thiqatech.

Outcome

  • The catalogue, cart, and checkout run in Arabic and English.
  • A customer can pay by card or on delivery.
  • Orders, shipping, and returns live in the same shop.
